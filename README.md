## ✨ The Experience

Music is more than sound — it's a mood.

**MusicXO** is a desktop music player designed to bring together music, atmosphere, and a beautifully crafted interface. Explore a personalised listening experience with immersive visuals and themes that make the player feel like yours.

## 🌌 Features

- 🎨 **Personalised Themes** — Explore different visual styles.
- 🎵 **Music Playback** — Enjoy your listening sessions in one desktop app.
- 🌈 **Immersive Interface** — A modern design built around your listening experience.
- 📃 **Lyrics Experience** — Follow lyrics when supported and available.
- 🎶 **Queue & Playlists** — Organise your listening sessions.
- 💿 **Distinct Player Styles** — Discover different looks and moods.
